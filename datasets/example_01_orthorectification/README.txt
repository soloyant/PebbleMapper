example_01_orthorectification -- a PebbleMapper example project
==============================================================

What      One photograph of a 0.84 x 0.84 m quadrat frame on a pebble beach,
          taken by hand at an angle, to try the Orthorectify tab on. The frame
          size is written on the picture. It is a small illustration (704 x 528
          px), not a measurement photograph.

images/example_01_orthorectification.jpg
    Orthorectify: pick the photograph, click the four outer corners of the
    frame in order, keep the side lengths at 0.84 m, and press Rectify. The
    rectified image and its sidecar (GSD, corners, frame width) are written
    next to it, ready for Detect in Quadrat mode.

For measurements on real quadrat photographs, see example_03_Etretat and
example_06_paper_validation.
